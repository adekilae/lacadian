Checking the grids against the stations

The network's stations are not used to draw the parish maps. They are used to check them. The Rainfall and Soil moisture layers each show a table underneath the map: gridMET rain against each station's gauge (daily and weekly correlation, ratio of totals, agreement on rain days), and SMAP soil moisture against the station sensors (rank correlation and difference). Treat the tables as a first test with short records, not proof.

What this site does not do

  • It gives no advice on when to plant, irrigate, spray or insure.
  • Parish values are averages. A field in a wet parish can be dry.
  • Gridded and satellite values are estimates; gauges and sensors are points. Neither is the truth for a particular field.
  • Layers not yet built show as dashed tabs. Nothing here is example data.
