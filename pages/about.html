About this prototype

This is an unofficial prototype for review. It was built from public data by a graduate student working with the LSU Soil Hydrology and Remote Sensing (SHaRE) Lab. It has not been reviewed or approved by the LaCADIAN network team.

Still to be decided

  • Who owns and hosts the site.
  • The name and branding.
  • The licence, including for the station data (stated as CC BY-NC 4.0, academic and non-commercial use).
  • Which layers stay, and who checks them before anyone relies on them.

Not for farm decisions

The maps show measurements and public datasets. They do not say when to plant, irrigate or spray, and they are not a flood map, a forecast or a drought declaration.
