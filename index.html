    How to read this

    Soil moisture is the volumetric water content of the soil (m³/m³) measured by a sensor at each depth. Soils differ, so a reading of 0.30 is wet at one station and dry at another. The map therefore compares each station's latest reading with that station's own record: "wetter than 70% of days recorded" means that 70% of the days since the station began reporting had a lower value. Stations with under 90 days of record are not ranked.

    A dry day is a day with under 1 mm of rain at the station. Dry days in a row are counted up to the last day with a rainfall reading. These are point measurements at the station, not averages over an area.
