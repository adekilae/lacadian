What this is

An unofficial prototype, built from public data by a graduate student for review by the LaCADIAN network team. The station maps use the network's public data. The parish maps use public data from NASA, USDA, NOAA-funded gridMET, the US Drought Monitor, FEMA and the European Commission's JRC. The stations are also used to check the gridded rainfall and the satellite soil moisture; the results are on the Methods page.

It does not tell anyone when to plant, irrigate or spray.
